## Scope and Scaling Notes

- **Chunk boundary:** Attacks cover relocation (`service/relocate`, `service/node/relocate.go`, `service/catalog/relocation.go`), fleet inspection and batch wiping (`service/fence/inspect.go`, `service/lab/fleet.go`, `service/s3store/batch.go`), and experiments `10.2-acknowledged-writes-survive`, `10.3-interrupted-relocation`, and `10.4-rebuild-the-fleet`. Every "Astra will try" bullet for 10.2–10.4 in `OUTLINE.md` section 5 is covered.
- **Scaling:**
  - `KILL-01`: runs `10.2-acknowledged-writes-survive -mutant ack-before-put` with `-syscall-kills 2 -random-kills 1` and `-portable` with `-random-kills 1` (scaled from 4/2 defaults to keep runtime short while still exercising every check path).
  - `KILL-02b`: targets up to **8** evenly spaced `fsync`/`fdatasync` calls across the eviction window (out of 18 total).
  - `KILL-03c`: targets **3** `fsync`/`fdatasync` calls inside `Claim` (first, middle, last of 18 total).
  - `KILL-04`: kills at **every** `catalog.db*` `fsync`/`fdatasync` (all 6 observed points × both `local-durable` and `store-durable` recovery modes = 12 runs).
  - `KILL-05`: targets **4** evenly spaced `pwrite64` calls per durability mode (8 total).
  - `FLEET-01a/b/c`: scaled from 500 (and 2,000) databases to **16** databases on real SeaweedFS S3 (exact 10.4 shape: claim + 4 `store-durable` writes + half rolled out to step 5).
  - `FLEET-02`: **12** databases on real SeaweedFS S3.
  - `FLEET-03a/b`: **8** databases (2 open writer processes × 4 open DBs) for the deterministic fencing test, and **16** databases (2 hammering writer processes vs 4 concurrent rebuild workers) for the live concurrent race.
  - `FLEET-04`: runs `10.4-rebuild-the-fleet` at `-databases 32 -writers 4 -capacity 8 -checker-pause 200ms` (scaled from 500/2,000 so total suite runtime stays ~1–2 minutes, well under the 10-minute target).
- **Cross-platform builds:** All three Go programs compile cleanly under `GOOS=darwin GOARCH=arm64` and `GOOS=darwin GOARCH=amd64` with `CGO_ENABLED=0` (Linux `strace` attacks are guarded by `trace.Supported`).

### Group 2: `relocation-faults` (`agent_work/ch10b/adversarial/relocation-faults/main.go`)

| ID | Claim attacked | Method | What a BROKEN result would mean |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `REL-01` | Skipping the fence step causes two writers to appear and must be caught by 10.3 (OUTLINE 10.3, must-be-able-to-fail). | Run `10.3-interrupted-relocation -mutant skip-fence -case 1`, `-mutant bogus`, and an in-process `SkipFence` dual-writer check with `fence.Audit`. | `skip-fence` passed instead of failing, `bogus` was accepted, or `SkipFence` failed to produce dual writers caught by `ErrBadChain`. |
| `REL-02a` | An interrupted `partial-put` upload left in S3 during relocation (final segment slot 4 or fence slot 5) never creates two authoritative copies and is never listed as a restore point (OUTLINE 10.3). | Inject `faultstore.PartialPut` at step 3 (`ShipFinal`) and step 6 (`Activate`), then call `Resume`, `Rollback`, and `fence.Inspect`. | `Resume`/`Rollback` proceeded past a damaged slot, two writers became writable, or `fence.Inspect` listed the partial upload as a restore point. |
| `REL-02b` | A complete fence left in S3 by `fail-after-put` followed by a `stale-list` hiding it during `Rollback` still cannot split brain (OUTLINE 10.3). | Inject `FailAfterPut` on fence slot 5, wrap `Rollback`'s coordinator store in `stale-list:log/:1`, then run clean `Resume`. | Stale `Rollback` overwrote or branched the lineage instead of being fenced (`ErrFenced`), or clean `Resume` failed to adopt slot 5. |
| `REL-03a` | Relocating a database with an open long reader pinning a WAL read mark cannot split brain or corrupt state (OUTLINE 10.3). | Hold a deferred read transaction open on `a/db-0001.db`, commit step 3 `local-durable` on `a`, then attempt `Run` and `Rollback` while the reader remains open. | `Drain` succeeded without the DB at rest, two nodes became writable, or `Rollback` split-brained while the reader held `-wal`/`-shm`. |
| `REL-03b` | Closing the long reader lets `Resume` complete the previously blocked relocation with every write preserved (OUTLINE 10.3). | Close the long reader from `REL-03a` (letting `sqlite3WalClose` checkpoint and delete `-wal`/`-shm`), then call `Resume` and verify at-rest SHA-256 and `user_version=3`. | `Resume` failed after the reader closed, lost step 3, or left `b`'s file diverging from S3. |
| `REL-04` | `Rollback` before the fence returns ownership cleanly to `a` and finishes the relocation even when the dead destination `b` stays down (10.3 rollback claim). | Stop relocation `a -> b` after step 4 (`Rebuild`), kill `b` and leave it down, call `Rollback`, then attempt `BeginRelocation(a -> c)` and a retry of `Rollback` after `b` restarts. | `Rollback` ran `a.Claim` before `b.Discard`, failed on dead `b` leaving `relocations.state=running` while `a` already held epoch 3, blocking new relocations and turning retry into `superseded` with orphaned `.staged`. |
| `REL-05a` | Even if an uncooperative source ignores its `.sealed` file after `b` activates, S3 fencing stops it (build-notes correction 1). | Delete `a/db-0001.sealed` after step 6 (`Activate`) and send `StoreDurable` write to `a`. | Unsealed `a` got a write accepted after `b`'s epoch-2 fence instead of failing with `fence.ErrFenced`. |
| `REL-05b` | A concurrent `Claim` takeover by a third node `c` during an interrupted relocation `a -> b` supersedes the relocation and fences both `a` and `b`. | Interrupt `a -> b` after step 3 and after step 6, run `c.Claim` (epoch 3), then call `Resume`/`Rollback` and attempt writes on `a`, `b`, and `c`. | `Resume`/`Rollback` failed to mark `superseded` (`ErrSupersededBy`), or `a`/`b` wrote to S3 after `c`'s fence. |
| `REL-05c` | After `b.Activate` fails at `recordShipped` (`SQLITE_BUSY`) following `PutIfAbsent` + `writeBelief`, `Resume` brings `catalog.databases` up to date (`location`, `last_shipped`, `schema_version`). | Lock `catalog.db` via a post-`PutIfAbsent` hook so `b.Activate` fails at `recordShipped`, release the lock, call `Resume`, and inspect `catalog.Get`. | `b.Activate`'s early return (`n.owned[id] == epoch`) skipped `recordShipped`, leaving `location=""`, stale `last_shipped`, and stale `schema_version` even after `relocations.state=done`. |
