## Scope and Scaling Notes

- **Outline coverage:** Every "Astra will try" bullet for Chapter 6 (6.1–6.4) in `OUTLINE.md` section 5 is covered (Chapter 6 had no second-SQLite-version / newer-driver bullet to skip).
- **Iteration scaling:** None needed. `BUSY-04` runs the full 100 re-runs of every Experiment 6.2 matrix cell (A, B, C1, C2, C3, D), keeping total suite runtime well under the 10-minute target on 4 cores / 7 GiB.
- **Portability:** Linux-only observation (`strace`, `/proc/locks`) is guarded at runtime; `GOOS=darwin go build ./...` compiles cleanly without cgo.
